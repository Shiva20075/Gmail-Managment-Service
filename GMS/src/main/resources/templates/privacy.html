Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

1. Introduction

Gmail Management Service (GMS) helps users organize Gmail messages through email categorization, AI-generated summaries, and email management actions.

2. Information We Access

With your authorization, GMS may access:

  • Your Google account email address and basic profile information.
  • Gmail messages and metadata required for processing.
  • OAuth tokens required to maintain authorized access.

3. How We Use Information

  • Classify emails as important, normal, or promotional/spam.
  • Generate concise summaries and email digests.
  • Provide supported Gmail management actions.
  • Authenticate users and maintain authorized access.

4. Third-Party Services

GMS may use the Google Gmail API to access and manage messages, the Anthropic Claude API to process email content, and Amazon Web Services (AWS) to host the application or store account data. Their respective privacy policies also apply.

5. Data Storage and Security

Depending on the implementation, GMS may store account information and OAuth refresh tokens in a database. Appropriate safeguards should be used to protect sensitive information.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Data should be retained only as long as necessary for the service and applicable legal requirements. You can revoke GMS access through your Google Account settings. For deletion requests, contact the service operator using the support address below.

7. Google API Services User Data Policy

GMS's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the service or its data practices change. Updates will be published on this page.
